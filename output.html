# One-Day Meal Plan for Vegetarian Endurance Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for muscle recovery and strength building that accommodates lactose intolerance and vegetarian preferences:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 2,800 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (85g), Carbs (380g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (120g), ground flaxseed (15g), and almond milk (240ml). Two scrambled eggs (100g) on the side.
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 65g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Hummus (60g) with carrot sticks (100g) and whole grain crackers (40g).
    * Protein: 6g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 35g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil and quinoa bowl (150g cooked lentils, 80g cooked quinoa) with roasted sweet potato (200g), spinach (80g), and tahini dressing (20g). Cherry tomatoes (80g).
    * Protein: 28g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Peanut butter (30g) with apple (180g) and sunflower seeds (20g).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 35g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked tofu (200g) marinated in soy sauce and ginger, with brown rice (100g cooked), steamed broccoli (250g), and sesame oil (10ml).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 80g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Coconut yogurt alternative (200g, dairy-free) with berries (100g) and granola (30g).
    * Protein: 7g, Fat: 9g, Carbs: 35g

This meal plan emphasizes plant-based protein sources (legumes, tofu, eggs, nuts, and seeds), complex carbohydrates for endurance, and healthy fats to support recovery and strength building while remaining fully vegetarian and lactose-free.
